## Introduction

GigHala is a peer-to-peer freelance marketplace built specifically for Malaysia. Every gig listed on the platform is verified for Syariah compliance using AI and the guidance of JAKIM (Jabatan Kemajuan Islam Malaysia). Whether you are a freelancer looking for work or a business seeking skilled talent, GigHala connects you securely and ethically.

**Key benefits at a glance:**

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| Halal-verified gigs | AI + JAKIM review on every listing |
| Secure payments | Escrow holds funds until work is approved |
| Fast payouts | 24-hour transfer to Touch 'n Go or bank account |
| Low platform fee | 15% on completed gigs |
| Malaysian focus | Malay/English, MYR, local locations |
| Gig worker protection | Automatic SOCSO contributions |

## Getting Started

### Creating an Account

1. Go to the GigHala homepage and click **Daftar / Register**.
2. Enter your **full name**, **email address**, and a strong **password** (minimum 8 characters with a mix of letters and numbers).
3. Select your primary role: **Pekerja (Freelancer)**, **Pelanggan (Client)**, or **Kedua-dua (Both)**.
4. Accept the Terms of Service and click **Cipta Akaun / Create Account**.
5. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the confirmation link.

> **Note:** You can switch between the freelancer and client role at any time from your dashboard without creating a new account.

### Verifying Your Email

After registration, GigHala sends an email from `noreply@gighala.com`. Click the **Verify Email** button in that message. The link expires after 24 hours — if it expires, log in and request a new one from the banner on your dashboard.

### Social Login

You can sign up or log in with an existing account from:

- Google
- Apple
- Microsoft
- Facebook
- X (Twitter)

Click the relevant button on the login page. Social login creates your GigHala account automatically using your existing credentials. No separate password is required.

### Setting Up Your Profile

A complete profile increases your chances of being hired and builds trust with other users.

1. Click your **avatar** in the top-right corner and select **Tetapan / Settings**.
2. Upload a clear **profile photo** (JPEG or PNG, max 5 MB).
3. Fill in your **bio**, highlighting your skills and experience.
4. Add your **location** (state and city in Malaysia).
5. Select your **skill categories** from the 27+ available categories (e.g., Reka Grafik, Pembangunan Web, Servis Rumah).
6. Add your **portfolio** — links or uploaded samples of past work.
7. Enter your **payout details** (bank account or Touch 'n Go eWallet) for receiving payments.
8. Click **Simpan / Save**.

## Understanding User Roles

GigHala supports three roles. A single account can hold all three.

| Role | Can Do |
|---|---|
| **Freelancer (Pekerja)** | Browse gigs, apply, submit work, receive payment |
| **Client (Pelanggan)** | Post gigs, review applications, release payment, leave reviews |

Switch your active role from the dashboard sidebar or the account menu.

## For Freelancers (Workers)

### Browsing Gigs

1. Click **Cari Kerja / Find Work** in the top navigation.
2. Use the filters to narrow results:
   - **Kategori** — e.g., Pemasaran Digital, Terjemahan, Pembinaan
   - **Lokasi** — state or city
   - **Bajet** — minimum and maximum in MYR
   - **Kemahiran** — specific skills required
   - **Tarikh tamat** — deadline range
3. Click any gig card to view the full description, budget, required skills, and client information.
4. Check the **Halal Verified** badge to confirm the gig has passed Syariah compliance review.

### Applying to a Gig

1. Open the gig detail page and click **Hantar Permohonan / Apply**.
2. Write your **cover letter** — explain why you are the right person, your relevant experience, and your approach.
3. Enter your **bid amount** in MYR. You may bid below, at, or above the posted budget, but provide a reason if you deviate significantly.
4. Set your **estimated delivery time**.
5. Optionally attach a **portfolio sample** or work examples relevant to this gig.
6. Click **Hantar / Submit**.

The client will receive a notification. You can track the status of your application from **Dashboard → Permohonan Saya / My Applications**.

### Video Pitches

Stand out from other applicants by submitting a 30-second video pitch alongside your written proposal.

1. On the application form, click **Rekod Video / Record Video**.
2. Allow browser access to your camera and microphone.
3. Record up to 30 seconds explaining your skills and enthusiasm for the project.
4. Review the recording and click **Guna Video Ini / Use This Video** or re-record if needed.
5. Submit your application as normal — the video is attached automatically.

### Managing Your Applications

From **Dashboard → Permohonan Saya**, you can see:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Menunggu** | Application submitted, awaiting client review |
| **Disenarai Pendek** | Client has shortlisted you as a strong candidate |
| **Diterima** | You have been selected — work can begin |
| **Ditolak** | Client selected another freelancer |

You will receive an email and in-app notification when your status changes.

### Submitting Completed Work

Once your application is accepted and you have finished the work:

1. Go to **Dashboard → Kerja Diterima / Accepted Gigs** and open the relevant gig.
2. Click **Hantar Kerja / Submit Work**.
3. Upload your deliverables (images, documents, ZIP files, links, etc.).
4. Add a short description of what you delivered and any notes for the client.
5. Click **Hantar / Submit**.

The client receives a notification to review your work. If the client requests revisions, you will receive their feedback and can re-submit.

### Getting Paid

After the client **approves** your submitted work:

1. The funds held in escrow are released to your **GigHala Wallet** within 24 hours.
2. A 15% platform fee is deducted automatically.
3. If applicable, a SOCSO contribution is calculated and recorded.
4. Transfer your wallet balance to your bank account or Touch 'n Go eWallet from **Tetapan → Dompet / Wallet**.
5. Withdrawals are processed within one business day.

### Building Your Portfolio

1. Go to **Tetapan → Profil → Portfolio**.
2. Click **Tambah Item / Add Item**.
3. Upload an image, PDF, or video, or paste a URL to an external project.
4. Give it a title and short description.
5. Save. Portfolio items appear on your public profile for clients to review.

## For Clients (Employers)

### Posting a Gig

1. Click **Cari Pekerja / Find Workers** or **Siarkan Kerja / Post a Gig** from the navigation.
2. Fill in the gig form:
   - **Tajuk** — a clear, descriptive title (e.g., "Design company logo in Islamic geometric style")
   - **Penerangan** — detailed description of the scope, requirements, and expected output
   - **Kategori & Kemahiran** — select the relevant category and required skills
   - **Bajet** — fixed price or range in MYR
   - **Tarikh Tamat** — deadline for the completed work
   - **Lokasi** — remote or specify a Malaysian state/city
   - **Foto Gig** — optional image to illustrate the project
3. Click **Siarkan Gig / Post Gig**.
4. The system automatically runs a **Halal Compliance Check** before publishing.

### Halal Compliance Check

GigHala uses an AI model (powered by Groq) cross-referenced with JAKIM guidelines to verify every gig before it goes live. The check reviews the title, description, and category for any content that would be impermissible under Islamic law (e.g., alcohol, gambling, adult content, riba-based financial services).

- **Lulus / Pass** — your gig is published immediately.
- **Gagal / Fail** — you receive specific feedback on which parts need to change. Edit the gig and resubmit.
- **Semakan Manual / Manual Review** — borderline cases are escalated to the GigHala moderation team within one business day.

### Reviewing Applications

1. From **Dashboard → Gig Saya / My Gigs**, click on the gig.
2. The **Permohonan / Applications** tab shows all submissions with:
   - Freelancer name, rating, and profile summary
   - Cover letter and bid amount
   - Portfolio samples and video pitch (if submitted)
3. You can:
   - **Senarai Pendek / Shortlist** — save promising candidates for later comparison
   - **Terima / Accept** — select this freelancer (see next section)
   - **Tolak / Reject** — decline the application with an optional note

You can shortlist multiple freelancers and accept one.

### Accepting a Freelancer

1. Click **Terima / Accept** next to the chosen applicant.
2. You will be prompted to **deposit the agreed amount into escrow**. This step protects both parties — the freelancer sees confirmed funds before starting work, and you retain control until the work is approved.
3. Complete the payment using your preferred method (see [Supported Payment Methods](#supported-payment-methods)).
4. The freelancer receives a notification and work begins.

### Reviewing Submitted Work

When the freelancer submits work, you will receive an email and in-app notification.

1. Go to **Dashboard → Kerja Diterima** and open the gig.
2. Review the uploaded deliverables.
3. Choose one of the following actions:
   - **Luluskan / Approve** — work meets the brief; payment is released from escrow.
   - **Minta Semakan / Request Revision** — provide written feedback on what needs to change. The freelancer can re-submit.
4. You have up to **7 days** to respond after a submission. If no action is taken, the system auto-approves and releases payment.

### Releasing Payment

Payment is released automatically when you approve the work. It is transferred from escrow to the freelancer's GigHala Wallet within 24 hours. You receive a **Receipt** confirming the transaction, accessible from **Dokumen / Documents**.

## Payments & Escrow

### How Escrow Works

Escrow is a neutral holding account managed by GigHala. It protects both parties:

- **For clients**: your money is only released when you are satisfied with the work.
- **For freelancers**: the client cannot cancel and keep the money once work has begun.

The escrow lifecycle:

```
Client posts gig
     ↓
Client accepts freelancer → Client deposits to escrow
     ↓
Freelancer works and submits
     ↓
Client reviews
     ↓ (approve)               ↓ (revision)
Payment released           Freelancer revises
to freelancer wallet           and resubmits
```

### Milestones

For larger or longer projects, you can split the project into **milestones** — smaller deliverable stages, each with its own payment.

1. When posting or editing a gig, click **Tambah Milestone / Add Milestone**.
2. Set the milestone name, description, amount, and due date.
3. Each milestone has its own escrow deposit, submission, and approval cycle.
4. Freelancers are paid per milestone as work progresses.

### Invoices & Receipts

- **Invoice** — generated by the freelancer when submitting work; confirms the amount owed.
- **Receipt** — generated by GigHala after payment is released; confirms payment was made.

Both documents are available under **Dokumen / Documents** in your dashboard and can be downloaded as PDFs.

### Wallet & Withdrawals

Your **GigHala Wallet** is your in-platform balance. Earnings from completed gigs appear here first.

To withdraw:
1. Go to **Tetapan → Dompet / Wallet**.
2. Click **Keluarkan / Withdraw**.
3. Select your saved payout account (bank account number or Touch 'n Go eWallet).
4. Enter the amount and confirm.
5. Funds arrive within one business day.

To add a payout account:
1. Go to **Tetapan → Pembayaran / Payment Settings**.
2. Click **Tambah Akaun / Add Account**.
3. Enter your bank details or link your Touch 'n Go eWallet.
4. GigHala sends a small verification deposit; confirm the amount to complete setup.

### Supported Payment Methods

| Method | Use Case |

| Credit / Debit Card | Depositing into escrow |
| FPX Online Banking | Depositing into escrow |
| Touch 'n Go eWallet | Depositing & receiving payouts |

All payment processing is Syariah-compliant.

## Fractional & Retainer Work

In addition to one-off gigs, GigHala supports ongoing work arrangements:

- **Fractional Work** — hire a freelancer for a set number of days per week (e.g., 2 days/week for 3 months).
- **Retainer** — a fixed monthly fee for a defined scope of ongoing services.

To post a fractional or retainer listing:
1. When creating a gig, change **Jenis Penyenaraian / Listing Type** to **Fractional** or **Retainer**.
2. Set the days per week (for fractional) or monthly rate (for retainer).
3. Set the contract duration.
4. Payments are processed on a recurring monthly cycle via the same escrow system.

## Messaging

GigHala includes an integrated chat system so clients and freelancers can communicate without sharing personal contact information.

1. Open any gig or application and click **Mesej / Message**.
2. A conversation thread opens in **Mesej / Messages** (accessible from the top navigation).
3. You can share text, files, and images within the conversation.
4. Notifications for new messages appear in the notification bell and are sent by email.

## Ratings & Reviews

After a gig is marked as complete, both the client and the freelancer are invited to leave a review.

1. Go to **Dashboard** and find the completed gig in the notification or history section.
2. Click **Tinggalkan Ulasan / Leave Review**.
3. Give a star rating from 1 to 5.
4. Write a short comment describing your experience.
5. Submit. Reviews are public and appear on both parties' profiles.

Reviews cannot be edited after submission. If you believe a review violates GigHala's guidelines, submit a report to Support.

**Rating summary** on each profile shows the average star rating, total number of reviews, and ratings broken down by specialization category.

## Account Settings & Security

### Identity Verification

Verified accounts display a **Disahkan / Verified** badge and can access higher-value gigs.

1. Go to **Tetapan → Pengesahan / Verification**.
2. Upload a clear photo of your **MyKad (IC)** or **Pasport**.
3. Take a **selfie** holding the document.
4. Submit for review. The GigHala team reviews submissions within one business day.

Your identity documents are encrypted using field-level encryption in compliance with Malaysia's **Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA)**.

### Two-Factor Authentication

Enable 2FA for additional login security.

1. Go to **Tetapan → Keselamatan / Security**.
2. Click **Aktifkan 2FA / Enable 2FA**.
3. Scan the QR code with an authenticator app (e.g., Google Authenticator, Authy).
4. Enter the 6-digit code from the app to confirm.
5. Save your backup codes in a safe place.

Once enabled, you will need to enter a one-time code from your authenticator app each time you log in from a new device.

### Privacy & PDPA

GigHala complies with the Malaysia Personal Data Protection Act 2010. Sensitive fields — phone numbers, IC/passport numbers, and bank account details — are encrypted at rest. You can request a copy of your data or request deletion from **Tetapan → Privasi / Privacy**.

## SOCSO Compliance (Gig Workers Bill 2025)

Under the Malaysia **Gig Workers Act 2025**, gig platform operators must facilitate SOCSO (Social Security Organisation / PERKESO) contributions for eligible workers.

GigHala handles this automatically:

1. When a gig payment is approved, the system calculates a **1.25% SOCSO contribution** from the gross payout.
2. The deduction is recorded on your earnings statement.
3. GigHala submits contribution data to PERKESO via the approved API on your behalf.
4. You can view and download your **monthly SOCSO statement** from **Dashboard → Penyata SOCSO / SOCSO Statement**.

SOCSO coverage provides access to Employment Injury Scheme benefits in case of workplace accidents.

> **Note:** SOCSO deductions apply only to Malaysian citizens and permanent residents earning above the threshold set by PERKESO.

## Disputes & Support

### Raising a Dispute

If a client refuses to release payment after work has been delivered, or if a freelancer delivers work that does not match the agreed brief, either party can raise a dispute.

1. Go to the relevant gig and click **Failkan Pertikaian / File Dispute**.
2. Describe the issue clearly and attach any supporting evidence (messages, files, screenshots).
3. The GigHala moderation team reviews both sides and makes a binding decision within 5 business days.
4. Funds in escrow remain frozen during the review.

### Contacting Support

- **In-app chat**: Click the **Sokongan / Support** link in the footer for live chat with the support bot, which escalates to a human agent if needed.
- **Support tickets**: Open a formal ticket from **Tetapan → Sokongan**. You will receive a ticket number and email updates on progress.
- **Email**: support@gighala.com

### Reporting a Gig

If you encounter a gig that you believe is not halal-compliant, illegal, or otherwise violates GigHala's guidelines:

1. Open the gig detail page.
2. Click the **Laporkan / Report** flag icon.
3. Select a reason and add details.
4. The moderation team investigates and responds within 24 hours.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is GigHala only for Muslims?**
A: GigHala is open to everyone. However, all gigs on the platform must comply with Syariah principles, which means certain types of work (e.g., alcohol promotion, adult content, gambling) cannot be listed.

**Q: How long does it take to get paid?**
A: Once a client approves your work, funds are released from escrow to your GigHala Wallet within 24 hours. Withdrawal to your bank or Touch 'n Go eWallet takes up to one additional business day.

**Q: What is the platform fee?**
A: GigHala charges a 15% platform fee on the total value of each completed gig. This is deducted from the freelancer's payment. There are no fees for posting gigs or applying.

**Q: What happens if the client disappears after I submit work?**
A: If a client does not respond to a work submission within 7 days, the system automatically approves the work and releases your payment from escrow.

**Q: Can I negotiate the price after applying?**
A: Yes. Use the in-app messaging system to discuss the scope and price with the client before they formally accept your application. The accepted bid amount is what gets deposited into escrow.

**Q: How do I cancel a gig?**
A: Before escrow is funded, either party can cancel without penalty. Once escrow is funded, cancellation may result in a partial refund depending on how much work has been completed. If there is a dispute, file a formal dispute for moderation team review.

**Q: Is my personal information safe?**
A: Yes. Sensitive personal data (IC number, bank account, phone number) is encrypted using field-level encryption. GigHala complies with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA 2010).

**Q: How do I delete my account?**
A: Go to **Tetapan → Privasi → Padamkan Akaun / Delete Account**. You must have no active gigs, open disputes, or pending payouts before deletion. Completed transaction records are retained as required by Malaysian financial regulations.

**Q: What categories of work are available?**
A: GigHala covers 27+ categories including:
Reka Grafik, Pembangunan Web, Pemasaran Digital, Penulisan & Terjemahan, Fotografi & Videografi, Pembinaan & Renovasi, Servis Rumah, Pendidikan & Tuisyen, Pengangkutan & Penghantaran, Kesihatan & Kecergasan, Masakan & Katering, Muzik & Hiburan Halal, IT & Sokongan Teknikal, Perunding Perniagaan, Undang-undang & Pematuhan, Kewangan & Perakaunan, dan banyak lagi.
